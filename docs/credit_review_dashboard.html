Credit risk case study · Akhand Raj

A credit model review on 589,249 real LendingClub loans

LendingClub's own loan grade is the incumbent model. I built two challengers from application data only, reviewed them out of time, traced why 2015 loans went bad more often, and back-tested underwriting rules. Every chart and control below runs on the tables produced by one reproducible run.

2015 loans went bad more often, and grade mix was not the reason

FINDING
Bad rate rose from 12.3% (2013) to 14.9% (2015). The grade mix got safer (−0.56 pp); loans of the same grade performing worse added +3.11 pp. Mix and within-grade effects add up to the total exactly.

What I would tell a risk committee

Model

Keep the grade, add the scorecard as an overlay

Gini +0.013 (95% CI +0.011 to +0.016). Recalibrate the intercept on the latest vintage first: all models under-predict 2015 by 1.4-2.0 pp.

Policy

Add a post-loan DTI cap of 35

Declines 21% of loans at a 20.6% bad rate, separates risk inside all 5 score quintiles, and lifts the kept book's return by 0.24 pp.

Monitoring

Watch FICO below 680 and renters

Within-grade bad rate up 4.5 pp and 4.2 pp. Mortgage-account CSI is 0.20, already in the watch band.

How it was built
  • SQL (DuckDB): text-first parsing, data-quality funnel, static pools, months-on-book curves and quarterly health with CTEs and window functions.
  • Scorecard: hand-written monotonic WoE binning, IV ≥ 0.02 and |correlation| ≤ 0.7 filters, coefficient sign checks, points at 600 / 50:1 / PDO 20. Highest VIF 1.92.
  • Challenger: HistGradientBoosting, early stopping (343 trees), monotone constraints on FICO, DTI, inquiries, utilisation and loan-to-income.
  • Review: Gini, KS, PSI, CSI, decile calibration and paired bootstrap confidence intervals on a 2015 out-of-time sample.
  • Strategy: swap-sets, cut-off tables with realised return, existing and post-loan DTI caps tested inside score quintiles.
  • 32 unit tests, including a leakage guard and a parser test against LendingClub's raw formats.
Limitations
  • Reject inference: only loans LendingClub approved are observed, so nothing here shows how the models rank declined applicants.
  • The grade uses bureau and platform data not in this file, so the comparison tests data as well as method.
  • 2012-2015 is one benign US credit cycle with no recession in sample.
  • US unsecured loans. For an Indian personal-loan book the method transfers (bureau score, FOIR, DPD buckets); the coefficients do not.
  • Realised return ignores servicing fees, funding cost and time value of money.
